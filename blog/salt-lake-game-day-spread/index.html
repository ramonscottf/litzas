Game Day in Salt Lake: Feeding the Whole Crew Without Missing a Play

The trick to hosting game day isn't fancy — it's getting everybody fed without you stuck in the kitchen missing every big play. Here's the Salt Lake game-day spread, sorted, sourced entirely from local favorites and built to feed a room.

The main event: pizza, obviously. A couple of large Litzas pies — one crowd-pleasing pepperoni, one loaded supreme or Meatza — feed a room and reheat like champs at halftime. The whole point of pizza on game day is that nobody has to babysit it: order ahead so it lands right at kickoff, and you're in your seat for the opening drive. Litzas has been doing hand-tossed, made-from-scratch pies since 1965, so this is a part of the day you can stop worrying about.

The wings. Every spread needs them. Wing Coop on Wasatch Boulevard is the local hero — fresh, never-frozen, and big on flavor, with a sauce list deep enough to keep everyone happy (the BBQ and garlic parmesan are the consensus picks, the honey habanero for the brave). Fans will tell you they're some of the best wings in the valley, full stop. Need a second option that's easy to order in bulk for a family crowd? Wing Nutz is a Utah-grown chain built for exactly this — build-your-own heat levels, kid-friendly, and simple to add to a catering run.

The drinks. Skip the beer run and do it the Utah way — a tray of dirty sodas keeps everyone, kids included, happy through overtime. Swig or FiiZ will batch a carrier of Dirty Dr Peppers and whatever custom combinations your crew is into, and FiiZ in particular tends to stay open seven days a week if your game lands on a Sunday. (Litzas also pours root beer, apple beer, lemonade, and fruit punch if you'd rather grab everything in one stop.)

The dessert. Stash a pint or two in the freezer before everybody arrives so the celebration — or the consolation — is ready the second the clock hits zero. Rockwell and Blacksmith both sell their super-premium pints to go, and a scoop of Rockwell Road on a win tastes even better than it sounds.

Let someone else cook. Feeding a bigger crew than usual? Litzas does group orders and catering, so the centerpiece of your spread shows up hot and handled. Build the rest of the table around it — wings, sodas, a couple of pints — and you've fed the whole room without missing a single snap.

That's the move: order the pizza ahead, round it out with local wings and dirty sodas, keep dessert on ice, and actually watch the game you're hosting.
